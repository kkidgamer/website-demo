# School Website Packages

> Draft tiering — adjust feature splits and pricing once real project costs (design hours, dev hours, hosting) are known.

## Overview

Three tiers, aimed at different school budgets and needs: a low-cost presence site, a standard interactive site, and a full-service package with ongoing support.

---

## 1. Starter Package — "Basic Presence"

**For:** Small schools that just need a professional online presence.

**Includes:**
- Up to 5 pages (Home, About, Admissions, Contact, Gallery)
- Mobile-responsive design
- School logo and branding integration
- Contact form
- Basic SEO setup (titles, meta descriptions)
- 1 round of revisions
- Domain + hosting setup assistance (client pays hosting/domain fees)

**Turnaround:** ~1–2 weeks

**Not included:** CMS access, blog, custom features, ongoing maintenance

---

## 2. Standard Package — "Interactive School Site"

**For:** Schools wanting a manageable, content-rich site they can update themselves.

**Includes everything in Starter, plus:**
- Up to 10–12 pages (adds: Academics, Staff Directory, News/Blog, Events Calendar, Downloads/Resources)
- Content Management System (CMS) so staff can edit text/images
- Photo gallery with categories (events, facilities, activities)
- News/announcements section
- Basic SEO + Google Business/Maps integration
- 2 rounds of revisions
- 1 training session for staff on using the CMS
- 3 months of free minor support (bug fixes, small text edits)

**Turnaround:** ~3–4 weeks

**Not included:** custom application features (e.g. online admissions portal, fee payment), multi-language support

---

## 3. Premium Package — "Full-Service School Platform"

**For:** Schools that want a comprehensive digital presence with functional tools, not just information.

**Includes everything in Standard, plus:**
- Unlimited core pages
- Online admissions/enquiry form with document upload
- Staff/parent portal login (basic — announcements, downloads)
- Events calendar with RSVP or registration
- Multi-language support (if needed)
- Custom design (not template-based)
- Advanced SEO (structured data, sitemap, analytics setup)
- 3 rounds of revisions
- Priority support
- 6–12 months of maintenance and hosting management included

**Turnaround:** ~6–8 weeks
